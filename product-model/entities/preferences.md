# Preferences

Choices kept on this device for listening, library organization and presentation.

## Information kept

- **Playback** — Quality, normalization, spatial audio, crossfade, preloading, silence and video preferences.
- **Autoplay** — Whether ordinary queues request further music.
- **Lyrics** — Enabled source order, timing preference and lyric presentation choices.
- **Translate lyrics** — Whether lyric translation is enabled.
- **Translation language** — Explicit target language or the phone language.
- **Car lyrics** — Whether media metadata carries lyrics and which display options apply.
- **Downloads** — Wi-Fi-only and automatic liked-song download choices.
- **Smart Downloads** — Whether predictive downloads are enabled.
- **Smart space** — Budget shared by predictive downloads and streamed audio cache.
- **Smart mobile data** — Whether Smart Downloads may use mobile data.
- **Home** — Greeting, shortcuts and section preferences.
- **Pinned playlists** — Playlists selected for Home, in their chosen order.
- **Library** — Default library tab, playlist order and Replay choices.
- **Local sections** — Visible local-file sections and their order.
- **Appearance** — Theme, typography and presentation preferences.
- **Navigation** — Start tab and navigation preferences.
- **Player** — Player presentation and control preferences.
- **Equalizer** — Enabled state, preset, band levels, bass and loudness settings.
- **Recent searches** — The most recent submitted search terms.
- **Music access** — Whether Android has granted access to local audio.
