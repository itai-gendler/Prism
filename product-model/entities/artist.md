# Artist

A catalog artist or a favourite artist kept on the phone.

## Information kept

- **Name** — Artist name.
- **Artwork** — Artist picture when available.
- **Sections** — Biography, releases, songs, videos and related artists returned for the artist.
- **Favourite** — The local favourite selection.
- **Channel** — Remote subscription destination when available.
