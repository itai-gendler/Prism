# Podcast show

A show returned by YouTube Music and browsed as episodes.

## Information kept

- **Title** — Show name.
- **Author** — Show author and any artist link.
- **Description** — Available show description and artwork.
- **Episodes** — Episodes returned for the show.
- **Saved** — Library bookmark returned by the service.
